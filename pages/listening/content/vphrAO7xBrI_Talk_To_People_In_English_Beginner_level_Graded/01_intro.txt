EN: Many language learners believe they need perfect grammar and a massive vocabulary before they can start speaking English, but this talk explains why that is a misconception. Through the story of a student named Elena, you will learn how one trip to London and the simple act of talking transformed her confidence. You will discover why the fear of making mistakes is often much larger than the actual challenge of communicating with others. This lesson also provides three practical, low-pressure strategies to help you begin speaking today, regardless of your current level. By the end of this talk, you will understand why focusing on your message rather than perfection is the real key to achieving fluency.
