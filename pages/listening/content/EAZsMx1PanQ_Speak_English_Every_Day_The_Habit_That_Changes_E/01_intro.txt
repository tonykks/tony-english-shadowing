EN: Many learners study English for years by memorizing vocabulary and grammar rules, yet they still feel nervous and unable to speak when the time comes to communicate. In this lesson, we will explore why knowing about English is not the same as being able to use it. We will identify the common habit that separates fast learners from those who remain stuck in a cycle of silence and frustration. You will discover practical methods to start speaking immediately, even as a beginner with a limited vocabulary. By learning how to transform simple daily activities into speaking practice, you can begin to rewire your brain for fluency. Stay with us as we uncover how to turn consistent, small actions into the confidence you need to speak English naturally.
