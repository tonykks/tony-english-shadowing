EN: Many English learners struggle because they focus heavily on memorizing grammar and vocabulary, yet still find their minds going blank during real conversations. This lesson addresses that common problem by explaining why you might be trying to speak before you have listened enough. We will explore how listening acts as the essential foundation for building natural speech, similar to how a child learns a language. You will discover why choosing easy English materials and using repetition are the most effective ways to train your brain. Finally, we will outline a simple 25-minute daily routine to help you transition from passive listening to confident speaking. Pay close attention to these strategies, as they are designed to change your daily practice and help you speak more naturally.
