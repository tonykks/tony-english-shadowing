[Part 3]
EN: Shadowing serves as a powerful technique where learners listen to and immediately mimic a speaker's rhythm and pronunciation. This process helps connect the eyes, ears, and mouth, allowing learners to understand how English sounds in real-life contexts without relying on complex grammar books.
