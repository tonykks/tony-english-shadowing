[Part 2]
EN: Impulsive reactions often lead to lasting guilt and severed connections that cannot be undone. The speaker emphasizes that while experiencing painful emotions is natural, reacting to them is a choice that can be managed through discipline. By labeling these feelings and intentionally waiting before responding, one can avoid the long-term regret of words spoken in anger.
KR: 충동적인 반응은 종종 되돌릴 수 없는 지속적인 죄책감과 끊어진 관계를 초래합니다. 연사는 고통스러운 감정을 경험하는 것은 자연스러운 일이지만, 그에 반응하는 것은 훈련을 통해 관리할 수 있는 선택의 문제라고 강조합니다. 이러한 감정에 이름을 붙이고 의도적으로 기다린 후 대응함으로써, 화가 나서 내뱉은 말로 인한 장기적인 후회를 피할 수 있습니다.

[Part 3]
EN: Silence is frequently mistaken for weakness, but it is actually a demonstration of dignity and self-control. By refusing to engage in unnecessary noise or defensive arguments, an individual maintains their focus and keeps their intentions hidden from those who seek to provoke them. Silence serves as a shield that allows the truth to emerge naturally over time.

[Part 4]
EN: Not every conflict or critic deserves an investment of your limited energy. The speaker warns that attempting to win every battle leads to emotional depletion and a loss of personal focus on one's own goals. Discernment is key; by walking away from situations that do not facilitate personal growth, one preserves their energy for what truly matters.
KR: 모든 갈등이나 비평가가 당신의 한정된 에너지를 투자할 가치가 있는 것은 아닙니다. 연사는 모든 싸움에서 이기려고 시도하는 것이 감정적 고갈과 자신의 목표에 대한 개인적 집중력 상실로 이어진다고 경고합니다. 통찰력이 핵심이며, 개인적인 성장을 촉진하지 않는 상황에서 벗어남으로써 진정으로 중요한 일에 에너지를 보존할 수 있습니다.

[Part 5]
EN: Calmness is a trained state of being that allows a person to remain steady even when faced with chaos or unreasonable people. By practicing calmness during minor daily inconveniences, one prepares their mind to handle larger life storms with grace. Staying calm is the mark of a leader, as it de-escalates conflict and allows for clearer decision-making.

[Part 6]
EN: Many people react reflexively due to past unhealed wounds rather than the needs of the present moment. By shifting from an automatic reaction to an intentional, conscious response, one can break these destructive cycles. [Part 7]
