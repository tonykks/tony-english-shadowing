EN: Many learners believe that fluency requires memorizing thousands of complex words, but this talk explains why that is a common misconception. The speaker shares their personal experience of breaking through a plateau by mastering a small, powerful group of only 50 simple words. You will discover how to use these everyday words to build sentences, think in English, and hold real conversations without needing perfect grammar. This lesson provides a practical, step-by-step method to stop translating in your head and start communicating confidently. By following these techniques, you can make your existing English knowledge more useful and turn your daily routine into an effective learning environment. Pay close attention to these small-step strategies, as they are designed to help you become a more comfortable and natural speaker.
