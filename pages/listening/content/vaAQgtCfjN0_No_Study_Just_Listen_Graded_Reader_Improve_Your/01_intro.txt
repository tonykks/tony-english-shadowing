EN: Many English learners study grammar rules and vocabulary for hours, yet still feel nervous and confused when trying to hold a real conversation. This lesson reveals that the secret to becoming fluent is not just studying harder, but training your ears to become familiar with the sound of the language. You will learn why trying to understand every single word often leads to frustration and how to avoid this common mistake. We will also explore why simple, repetitive listening is more effective than difficult, advanced materials for building your confidence. By the end of this talk, you will understand how to turn your daily routine into an English classroom and how to follow a 30-day challenge that will naturally change how you process the language.
