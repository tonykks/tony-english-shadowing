EN: Many students study grammar and vocabulary for years but still feel unable to speak when a real conversation begins. This lesson explores why textbook learning often fails to translate into natural communication and how to bridge that gap. You will learn why repeating short, authentic conversations is more effective than memorizing isolated words. By understanding the rhythm of listening, thinking, and answering, you can train your brain to retrieve language more quickly. We will also discuss how to handle mistakes without fear and why focusing on real-life topics makes your English feel alive. Prepare to discover how even a two-minute daily practice can transform your ability to connect with others.
