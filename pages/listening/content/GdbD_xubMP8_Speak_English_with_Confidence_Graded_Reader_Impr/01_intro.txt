EN: Many English learners feel their minds go blank and hearts race when they try to speak, even if they study every day. This lesson explores why your brain struggles to juggle translating and speaking simultaneously and why perfectionism often hinders your progress. By shifting your focus from being flawless to simply communicating, you will learn how to build confidence like a muscle. We will cover simple phrases that buy you time during conversations and provide practical strategies to turn mistakes into opportunities for growth. This guide is designed to help you stop waiting for perfect English and start speaking with confidence today.
