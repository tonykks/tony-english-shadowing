[Part 2]
EN: Elena explains her personal journey of starting with simple tasks, such as naming household objects and practicing conversations with herself in the mirror. [Part 3]
EN: The focus shifts to reading easy English stories aloud to build confidence and fluency, noting that repetition leads to better word retention. Additionally, Elena introduces the 'shadowing' technique, which involves mimicking a native speaker's tone and pace immediately after hearing them, to improve pronunciation and natural intonation.

[Part 4]
EN: Elena highlights the effectiveness of talking to oneself throughout the day to build mental connections in English. She also explains how to use technology smartly by changing device settings to English, using voice-typing for pronunciation checks, and utilizing apps and subtitles to create a personal, low-stress classroom environment.

[Part 5]
EN: The narrative emphasizes that small, daily habits are more effective than long, sporadic study sessions. By integrating simple English practices—like labeling furniture, speaking during chores, or reading for five minutes before bed—learners can make English a natural part of their daily life and avoid the frustration of force-studying.
KR: 이 이야기는 짧고 간헐적인 공부 시간보다 작고 매일 하는 습관이 더 효과적임을 강조합니다. 가구에 라벨 붙이기, 집안일 하며 말하기, 자기 전 5분 동안 책 읽기와 같은 간단한 영어 연습을 통합함으로써 학습자는 영어를 일상의 자연스러운 부분으로 만들고 억지로 공부하는 좌절감을 피할 수 있습니다.

[Part 6]
