[Card 1]
headword: burn out
part_of_speech: verb
meaning_kr: 녹초가 되다
definition_en: to become extremely tired or sick because you have worked too hard for a long time
definition_kr_literal: 오랜 기간 너무 열심히 일해서 극도로 지치거나 병이 나는 것
example_en: They burn out, sacrifice their health, and kill their happiness just to earn more money.
example_kr_literal: 그들은 더 많은 돈을 벌기 위해 번아웃되고, 건강을 희생하며, 행복을 죽입니다.
