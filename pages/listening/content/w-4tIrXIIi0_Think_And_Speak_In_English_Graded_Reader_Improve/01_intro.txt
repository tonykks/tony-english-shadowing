EN: Many language learners struggle with slow speaking, often because they try to translate every sentence in their heads before speaking. This lesson explores the concept of the 'translation trap' and why it creates a mental bottleneck that exhausts your limited working memory. By analyzing the story of a student named Marco, you will understand how translating acts like a difficult math problem that prevents natural conversation. You will also learn why thinking directly in English is a skill that can be acquired through simple, daily habits. Paying attention to this talk will provide you with five practical methods to stop translating and start thinking in English more naturally. Ultimately, this lesson aims to help you gain speed and confidence by showing you that you do not need perfect grammar or a massive vocabulary to speak fluently.
