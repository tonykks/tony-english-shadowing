EN: In this listening exercise, we follow the story of a 35-year-old woman named Cynthia who reflects on the breakdown of her ten-year marriage to her husband, Howard. Throughout the narrative, she details her attempts to communicate, her efforts to save their connection, and the heart-wrenching moment she discovers proof of his infidelity. Pay close attention to how Cynthia navigates the difficult transition from trying to salvage her relationship to prioritizing her own peace and self-worth. By listening to her journey, you will explore themes of loneliness, trust, and the courage required to make life-changing decisions when love no longer feels enough.
