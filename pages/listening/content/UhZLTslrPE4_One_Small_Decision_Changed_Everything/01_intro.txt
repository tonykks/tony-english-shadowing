EN: In this listening lesson, you will hear a story about a doctor with over 67 years of experience who faces a mysterious patient named John. John visits the clinic repeatedly, complaining of persistent weakness and fatigue despite taking various medicines. As the doctor searches for the source of his illness, she begins to suspect that his physical symptoms are not caused by a disease. You will follow their conversation as they explore whether the patient’s true problem lies in his body or his high-pressure lifestyle. This talk highlights a critical realization about the difference between physical sickness and the exhaustion caused by modern life. Pay close attention to the turning point in their dialogue to understand why the doctor eventually refuses to provide more medication. You will learn how the doctor helps John distinguish between the illusion of strength and the necessity of rest.
