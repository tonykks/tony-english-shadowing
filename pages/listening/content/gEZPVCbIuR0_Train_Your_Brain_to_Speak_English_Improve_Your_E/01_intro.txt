EN: Many students often feel discouraged because they can understand English well but struggle to speak it when the time comes. This lesson explores why your brain might be relying on old habits, such as translating every word, instead of processing English directly. You will learn how your brain functions like a garden, needing regular, simple practice to grow strong connections. We will look at why perfectionism can hinder your progress and how small, daily actions create better results than long study sessions. By the end of this talk, you will have practical exercises to train your brain to think in English and build natural fluency. Please pay close attention to the stories shared, as they reveal why consistency is the true secret to becoming a more confident speaker.
