EN: Many learners struggle to speak English fluently because they spend months studying grammar and vocabulary while waiting for the perfect moment to start talking. This lesson explores why you do not need perfect grammar, difficult words, or even a partner to begin improving your communication skills. By dedicating just 30 minutes each day to speaking, you can train your brain to find words faster and build lasting confidence. We will walk through practical techniques for practicing alone, choosing simple topics, and overcoming the fear of making mistakes. Stay with us until the end to learn how to build a consistent habit that transforms your English speaking from a stressful task into a natural part of your daily life.
