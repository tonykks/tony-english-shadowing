EN: The question "Tell me about yourself" is perhaps the most common opening in interviews, yet many people struggle to answer it effectively. In this lesson, we will explore why simply "being yourself" often leads to rambling and why having a clear, organized structure is essential for success. You will learn the "present, past, future" method, which helps keep your answers concise, engaging, and easy for an interviewer to follow. We will also discuss how to avoid common mistakes like talking too much or sounding like a memorized script. By the end of this talk, you will have the tools to turn this stressful question into an opportunity to speak with confidence.
