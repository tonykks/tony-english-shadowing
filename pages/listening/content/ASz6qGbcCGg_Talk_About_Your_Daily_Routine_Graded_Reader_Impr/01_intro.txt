EN: Many language learners struggle to describe their daily life because they rely on textbooks rather than their own experiences. In this lesson, we will learn how to describe your daily routine from the moment you wake up until you go to bed, using natural, real-world English. You do not need to memorize hundreds of words or complex grammar rules to become a confident speaker. Instead, we will focus on building your story step by step with practical phrases and small daily habits. By the end of this talk, you will be able to describe your day clearly and speak about your life with confidence. Let's start building your English fluency by talking about the things you actually do every single day.
