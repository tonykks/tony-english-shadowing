EN: Many language learners struggle with the 'translation trap,' a habit of mentally converting every thought from their native language into English before speaking. This two-step process often creates a mental bottleneck that leads to slow, hesitant speech and unnecessary frustration. In this lesson, you will learn why this approach hinders your fluency and how to train your brain to think directly in English instead. By following practical strategies like talking to yourself and using circumlocution, you can move away from relying on translation. We will also examine the case of a student named Marco to show how small, daily habits can lead to significant breakthroughs. Prepare to discover how to embrace mistakes and prioritize fluency over perfection in your language learning journey.
