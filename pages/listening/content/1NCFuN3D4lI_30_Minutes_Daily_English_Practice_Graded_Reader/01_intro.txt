EN: Many English learners focus heavily on grammar and vocabulary lists, yet they often feel frustrated when they cannot speak fluently. This lesson explains why one simple, often overlooked habit—reading in English for 30 minutes every day—is the key to unlocking your speaking ability and building natural confidence. You will discover how daily reading fills your mind with sentence patterns and vocabulary, making communication feel much easier over time. We will also explore practical tips on choosing the right materials and maintaining consistency without feeling overwhelmed by difficulty. Pay close attention to these insights, as they show how small, daily efforts can completely transform your English skills and change the way you perceive the learning process.
