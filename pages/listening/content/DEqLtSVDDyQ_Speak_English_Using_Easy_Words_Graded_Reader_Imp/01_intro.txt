EN: Many English learners feel stuck because they believe they need a massive vocabulary or perfect grammar to speak fluently. In this lesson, we explore why using simple, easy words is actually the fastest and most effective way to communicate in real-life situations. You will learn how to build confidence by using the English you already know to describe your world, handle daily tasks, and connect with others. We will practice short, practical dialogues and discover how small, consistent habits can train your brain to think directly in English. Pay close attention to these techniques, as they will help you overcome the fear of making mistakes and show you that you are already capable of speaking English today.
