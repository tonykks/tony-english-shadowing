EN: Many language learners struggle because they constantly try to translate between their native language and English, which can make the process feel slow and tiring. In this lesson, we will explore the powerful technique of "brain talking," which involves speaking to yourself in English within your own mind. By using simple sentences, naming objects, and asking yourself questions throughout the day, you can train your brain to think in English naturally. We will cover practical tips such as repeating what you hear, picturing words in your mind, and practicing in various daily situations. You will learn how this method helps you build confidence and speak more fluently without the fear of making mistakes. This practice requires no special equipment, just your own mind, and it is a habit you can start building today.
